Exploring a Topic

In this step, you explore your topic to build a general understanding of the field before narrowing it down into a specific research direction. The goal is to become familiar with key concepts, common terminology, and possible sub areas.

The links below lead to course materials related to this stage. Please read them before using the GenAI activity on this page, since the activity assumes that you are already familiar with the basic content. A basic understanding is also necessary to use GenAI appropriately and critically.

Using GenAI to clarify concepts and relationships in your topic map

If you want to use GenAI at this stage, use it only after you have completed your topic map. The aim is not to generate the map itself, but to help you clarify selected concepts and relationships within it.

This activity helps you make your topic map more explicit in words. In particular, it helps you identify what kind of concept something is and what kind of relationship exists between concepts. This can make it easier to move from broad topic exploration to a more focused research direction.

At this stage, GenAI can help you clarify what you mean, suggest reasonable concept or relationship labels, and improve the wording of your own explanations without changing the meaning. It should not generate your full topic map, decide your research direction, or formulate your central research question for you.

The preset prompts below are designed to support this process. Use them to make your reasoning more explicit, and always check whether the output matches your articles and your intended meaning.

If GenAI is enabled for you, you can use the preset prompts below. They are designed to guide your reasoning through questions, not to produce a final answer for you.

Using the preset prompts

The preset prompts are designed to structure your interaction with GenAI. They are not templates for a final answer, but tools to guide your reasoning process.

You will find two types of prompts below: a Course preset prompt and an Activity preset prompt. They serve different purposes.

Practical use
1. Copy the Course preset prompt first.
2. Add the Activity preset prompt below it.
3. Insert your own research idea where indicated.
4. Use the output critically and refine your work based on the logic.
