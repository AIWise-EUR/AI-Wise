Using GenAI in AWSI

Final Check of your paper using the Rubric

In this activity, you go through your paper using the rubric as a checklist. Instead of trying to improve everything at once, you focus on one criterion at a time and check whether your paper sufficiently meets that requirement.

With GenAI, the goal is not to rewrite your work, but to support this checking process. For each criterion, you provide the relevant part of your paper and ask GenAI to determine whether a substantial issue is present.

Importantly, GenAI's feedback is based on general patterns. This means it produces responses that are typically reasonable, but not necessarily the most precise judgment for your paper. The final evaluation should be based on a combination of your own understanding of the rubric and GenAI's feedback.

GenAI is particularly useful for detecting clear and substantial issues, rather than refining your paper to the highest possible level. For more nuanced improvements, discuss your work with peers, tutors, or the course coordinator.

Using the preset prompts

The preset prompts are designed to structure your interaction with GenAI. They are not templates for a final answer, but tools to guide your reasoning process.

You will find two types of prompts below: a Course preset prompt and an Activity preset prompt. They serve different purposes.

Practical use
1. Copy the Course preset prompt first.
2. Add the Activity preset prompt below it.
3. Insert your own research idea where indicated.
4. Use the output critically and refine your work based on the logic.
